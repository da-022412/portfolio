### Overview

MasterTax provides payroll tax technology for employers and service providers managing complex compliance requirements. Its website needed to communicate that offering through a clear, confident digital experience, connecting a detailed product story with the needs of prospective customers.

### The Challenge

The project brought together a precise visual design and content delivered through Sitecore. The challenge was to turn those requirements into a cohesive website, with a front end that could faithfully express the brand while accommodating the realities of CMS-driven content and responsive layouts.

### My Contribution

Working with iMedia Inc. as the primary Front End Engineer, I led the implementation of the Next.js front end and its integration with Sitecore. My responsibility spanned the presentation layer and the way content reached it, keeping the design intent and the technical implementation aligned throughout the build.

I defined the front-end data contract for the Sitecore integration, documenting the content structures and fields the interface required. This established a shared understanding of how CMS content would be delivered and rendered, giving the integration a clear foundation.

Using MUI and CSS-in-JS, I developed a custom component system tailored to the visual design. I carried the approved layouts into a high-fidelity implementation, working through typography, spacing, responsive behavior, and interaction details as parts of a consistent experience across the site.

### The Result

The finished website brought the visual design and Sitecore content together in a cohesive Next.js experience. The work combined a closely matched design implementation with a defined content integration, creating a reusable foundation for presenting the MasterTax offering and supporting ongoing publishing.
