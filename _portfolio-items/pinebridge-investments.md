### Overview

PineBridge Investments is a global asset manager with a wide range of funds and financial products. The challenge was to create a modern, scalable marketing site that could handle large sets of dynamic financial data while maintaining a seamless editorial workflow for non-technical users.

### Role

Front End Developer
Next.js, TailwindCSS, GraphQL, Contentful, ApexCharts

### The Problem

The existing setup made it difficult for editors to update fund data and for users to navigate PineBridge’s wide product range. With constantly changing market information, the site needed a way to surface data that was both reliable and easy to explore without overwhelming users.

### My Contribution

I built the front end from the ground up, taking Zeplin designs and translating them into pixel-perfect, responsive interfaces in Next.js and TailwindCSS. I implemented Contentful as the CMS, setting up GraphQL queries to pull in content and ensure editors could update fund information without developer involvement.

For the financial data, I developed interactive charts using ApexCharts, pulling live updates from Snowflake and rendering them dynamically. This allowed users to compare fund performance, view allocations, and explore data through filtering and sorting interfaces I built specifically for PineBridge’s product range.

The result was a site where complex financial data could be updated in real time and visualized in a way that was intuitive for both institutional and intermediary audiences.
